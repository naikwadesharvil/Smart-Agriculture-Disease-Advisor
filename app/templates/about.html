About Smart Agriculture Disease Advisor

Smart Agriculture Disease Advisor is an Artificial Intelligence powered web application that detects plant diseases from leaf images using a Convolutional Neural Network (CNN). It helps farmers, researchers and students quickly identify diseases and provides treatment and prevention recommendations.

Project Highlights

Deep Learning

Uses a Convolutional Neural Network trained on the PlantVillage dataset for accurate disease prediction.

14 Crop Types

Supports disease detection across multiple crops including Tomato, Potato, Apple, Corn, Grape and many more.

38 Disease Classes

Detects healthy plants as well as common fungal, bacterial and viral diseases.

Features

  • AI-powered disease detection
  • Upload JPG, JPEG and PNG images
  • Confidence score for predictions
  • Top-5 prediction analysis
  • Disease symptoms
  • Treatment recommendations
  • Organic farming solutions
  • Prevention guidelines
