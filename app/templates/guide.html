User Guide

Follow these simple steps to diagnose plant diseases using our AI-powered Smart Agriculture Disease Advisor.

How to Use the Application

Open the Home Page

Launch the Smart Agriculture Disease Advisor in your web browser.

Upload a Leaf Image

Choose a clear image of a plant leaf in JPG, JPEG or PNG format.

Click "Analyze Plant"

The AI model processes the uploaded image and predicts the disease.

View Detailed Results

Review confidence score, symptoms, treatments, prevention methods and disease timeline.

Best Practices

Good Lighting

Capture leaf images in natural daylight for better accuracy.

High Quality Image

Use sharp, focused images without blur or excessive shadows.

Single Leaf

Upload one leaf at a time with the affected area clearly visible.

Tips for Better Predictions

  • Capture only the affected leaf whenever possible.
  • Avoid blurry or low-resolution images.
  • Ensure the leaf occupies most of the image.
  • Avoid complex or distracting backgrounds.
  • Use the provided recommendations as guidance and consult agricultural experts for severe infections.
