Plant Disease Detection Guide

Follow these steps for the best AI-based prediction

Step 1

Capture a Clear Leaf Image

Take a clear photograph of the plant leaf showing the visible symptoms.

  • Use good lighting.
  • Keep the leaf clearly visible.
  • Avoid excessive blur.
  • Avoid images with unnecessary objects covering the leaf.
Step 2

Upload the Image

Go to the Home page and upload the plant leaf image.

  • Supported format: JPG
  • Supported format: JPEG
  • Supported format: PNG
Upload Image

CNN Deep Learning

The uploaded image is resized and normalized before being processed by the trained CNN model.

Step 3

AI Analysis

The CNN analyzes the visual features of the leaf and predicts the most likely disease class.

The system also calculates a confidence score and displays the Top-5 predictions.

Step 4

Understand the Result

After analysis, the result page provides detailed information about the predicted disease.

  • Disease name
  • Crop information
  • Scientific name
  • Pathogen information
  • Symptoms and causes
  • Treatment options
  • Prevention methods
  • Risk and severity information

Disease Report

Generate a PDF report containing the prediction and available disease information.

Step 5

Download Your Report

After receiving the prediction, use the Download Report option to generate a PDF report.

Tips for Better Predictions

Good Lighting

Use a well-lit image so that leaf symptoms are clearly visible.

Clear Image

Avoid blurry or extremely low-quality images.

Focus on the Leaf

Keep the affected leaf clearly visible in the photograph.

Important

AI predictions are intended as decision-support information. For serious crop disease problems, confirm the diagnosis with a qualified agricultural professional before applying treatments or chemicals.
