Image Guidelines

Good quality images improve prediction accuracy.

✅ Good Images

  • ✔ One leaf only
  • ✔ Natural daylight
  • ✔ Plain background
  • ✔ Entire leaf visible
  • ✔ Sharp focus

❌ Avoid

  • ✖ Blurry photos
  • ✖ Dark lighting
  • ✖ Multiple leaves
  • ✖ Hands covering leaf
  • ✖ Low resolution
